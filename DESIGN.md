# Gethuk Lindri Blauran | Design Direction v5.0
Decision: **Hybrid A+B**, approved 2026-10-17.

## A: Majapahit Golden Hour (primary)
Warm editorial food photography/product focus, atmospheric sunset, temple silhouette, cream product surfaces.
- Hero: sunset sky, temple silhouette, restrained batik-cloud motif.
- Catalog: quiet cream background; prominent product image, price and purchase action.
- Product modal and cart: high contrast, clear controls and information.

## B: Batik Nusantara Contemporary (accent)
Bold maroon batik, heritage ornaments, asymmetrical editorial layouts.
- Story section: distinct dark/maroon batik art direction.
- Footer: refined batik accent, not another copy of the hero.

## Explicit anti-repetition rule
Each section shares brand tokens but uses a distinct composition and illustration. Do not repeat the same background art on adjacent sections. No random stars, generic gradient blobs, emoji icons, or decoration that hides product details.

## Interaction language
- Product focus: purposeful 250–420ms transition.
- Cart: drawer with quantity controls and immediate totals.
- Checkout: review before copy; never imply WhatsApp was sent.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion. No autoplay motion that blocks ordering.

## Design review
Huashu: three distinct design directions and HTML prototyping.
UI UX Pro Max: searchable inspiration and UI guidelines, verify reference applicability.
Impeccable: critique, audit, polish, harden and responsive review.
Human review and actual browser checks are mandatory; tool names alone are not proof of quality.

## Release gate
Review desktop + mobile, keyboard, focus, contrast, empty cart, quantity boundaries, notes overflow, currency totals, reduced motion, loading and checkout copy. Run CI, inspect Draft PR, deploy preview, then request approval.
